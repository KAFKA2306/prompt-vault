# Anti-patterns

Reject equal-weight card walls when a table or sequence is clearer; pastel fill on every card; sparkles, robots, brains, magic wands, generic AI stars, and unexplained gradients; character art beside metrics, official rulings, warnings, investment decisions, or destructive actions; status conveyed only by color, icon, animation, position, or blur; image-derived facts without sources; sub-14px persistent metadata; mutable CDN dependencies; hidden provenance; and claims that a metric gate implies deployment readiness.
