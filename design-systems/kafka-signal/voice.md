# Voice guide

Use concrete verbs and domain nouns. Prefer `比較する`, `根拠を開く`, `履歴を見る`, `再検証する`, and `棄却する`.

State `未確認`, `推定`, `データなし`, `期限切れ`, `競合`, and `再現失敗` directly. Name the missing artifact and next mechanical action.

Do not use generic AI marketing, anthropomorphic loading/errors, baby-talk, celebratory performance language, or decorative emoji in application chrome. Material generated content must expose source, generation time, status, and correction/rejection path.
